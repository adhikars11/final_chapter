\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Green labor reallocation stalls from two-sided coordination failure
  \item Coordinated policy dominates: 39bp lower unemployment, 5bp fiscal cost, 23bp welfare
  \item Optimal policy is dynamic: rotate worker-firm-worker as constraints shift
  \item Transition welfare-improving only when environmental benefits exceed 0.87\% CEV
  \item Policy design, not just scale, determines efficiency and equity of transition
\end{itemize}
