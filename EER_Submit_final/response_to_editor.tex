\noindent \textbf{Reply to the Editor of the manuscript ``Resolving Coordination Frictions in Green Labor Transitions: Minimizing Unemployment, Costs, and Welfare Distortions.''}

\bigskip

Dear Editor,

\medskip

Thank you very much for your letter and for the opportunity to revise and resubmit my manuscript to the \textit{European Economic Review} special issue on Climate. I am grateful for the detailed reports from both referees and for the Associate Editor's recommendation.

I have revised the manuscript carefully to address the referee reports, with particular attention to the substantive concerns raised by Reviewer 1. I also revised the framing and interpretation of the transition and welfare results to ensure that the paper's claims are tightly aligned with the model structure.

Before turning to your specific request, I summarize the major revisions:

\textbf{First}, I strengthened the transition analysis and interpretation by clarifying the sequence-of-steady-states design, adding transition diagnostics, and tightening language around policy dynamics.

\textbf{Second}, I expanded the calibration and identification section with clearer moment-to-parameter mapping and additional sensitivity discussion/diagnostics.

\textbf{Third}, I implemented all textual and presentation revisions requested by the referees, including clarification of key calibration statements, improved figure interpretation, and stronger framing of policy magnitudes and limitations.

Taken together, these revisions substantially improve clarity, transparency, and the credibility of the quantitative policy conclusions.

Overall, I believe the revised manuscript directly addresses the concerns raised in the review process and is now significantly stronger.

I now move to your specific request.

\bigskip
\bigskip

\textbf{Comment 1: ``While the paper addresses an interesting question, the reports -- especially of Reviewer 1 -- raise a number of substantive concerns that need to be addressed before the manuscript could be considered further. I hope you can address them all in your revisions.''}

\medskip

Thank you. I have addressed all comments raised by both referees in the revised submission package.

Specifically:
\begin{itemize}
\item I provide a complete point-by-point response to Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2 in separate response letters.
\item For Reviewer 1's essential points, I revised the identification discussion and added transition evidence/diagnostics to support the interpretation of policy paths.
\item For Reviewer 2's concerns, I rephrased claims about dynamic optimality to match the sequence-of-steady-states framework, repositioned the main contribution toward policy-composition mapping along the transition, and expanded welfare robustness discussion (including risk-aversion sensitivity).
\item I also implemented all smaller comments regarding wording, consistency, figure presentation, and framing.
\end{itemize}

In the revised manuscript and response letters, I indicate where each change was made and how each concern was addressed.

\bigskip

Thank you again for the opportunity to revise this manuscript. I appreciate the constructive editorial guidance and consideration of the revised submission.
